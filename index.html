Travel

Fly into GSO (closest) or CLT. From GSO, take a car, taxi, or rideshare 30 minutes to Graylyn Estate. For those driving or renting a car, parking is available all weekend at Graylyn Estate.

We'd love for as many of our guests as possible to stay at Graylyn Estate. Having everyone together on-site will make the weekend feel especially festive and give us more opportunities to spend time together throughout the celebrations.

Graylyn Estate has rooms reserved for guests Thursday through Sunday under the Imus-Gardner wedding. Please email jobes@wfu.edu or call 336-758-2428 with your name, phone number, and a good time for Scott Jobe to call you and collect your payment information.

Children under 16 outside of family won't be included in the ceremony or reception, but are welcome to stay at Graylyn with their families throughout the weekend. Let us know if you plan to bring children so we can help explore childcare options.
