We'd love for as many of our guests to stay at Graylyn as possible, Thursday–Sunday. Having everyone together on-site will make the weekend feel especially festive and give us more opportunities to spend time together throughout the celebrations.

As of right now, children under 16 won't be included in the ceremony or reception. Children are welcome to stay at Graylyn with their families throughout the weekend, and we're planning to explore childcare options for wedding events that don't include children. Please feel free to reach out with any questions about your family's specific situation — we're happy to chat!
